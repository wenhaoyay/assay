# Frequently asked questions

**Can I use Device Alpha outdoors?** No. Device Alpha is for indoor use only.

**How many devices can Device Alpha control?** Up to 32 paired devices.

**Where do I find my serial number?** On the base of the device, or in the Acme Home app under Settings > About.

**Does Acme sell refurbished devices?** Acme does not sell refurbished devices.

**Can I transfer my warranty if I sell my device?** Yes, the warranty follows the device, not the owner.
