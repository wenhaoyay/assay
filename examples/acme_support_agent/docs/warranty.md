# Warranty policy

## Standard warranty
Every Acme device has a standard warranty that starts on the purchase date: 24 months for Device Alpha, Device Gamma and Device Beta Pro, 12 months for Device Beta. Accessories have a 12-month warranty.

## Acme Care+
Acme Care+ extends the standard warranty by 12 months. It costs $29 per device and must be bought within 30 days of the device purchase. Acme Care+ is not sold in the Southmark region.

## What is not covered
The warranty does not cover physical damage from drops, damage from unauthorised repairs, or normal battery wear. Water damage is not covered, except on Device Beta, whose IPX7 rating is covered.

## Making a claim
To make a claim you need the serial number, which is printed on the base of the device and shown in the Acme Home app under Settings > About.
