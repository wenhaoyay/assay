# Regional policies

Acme sells in three regions: Northvale, Southmark and Eastport.

## Northvale
Return window: 30 days. Standard shipping is free on orders over $50.

## Southmark
Return window: 14 days. Shipping is a flat $7 per order. Acme Care+ is not available.

## Eastport
Return window: 45 days. Standard shipping costs $5, and express shipping is not offered.
