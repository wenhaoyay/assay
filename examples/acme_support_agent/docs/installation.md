# Setting up Device Alpha

1. Connect Device Alpha to power with a USB-C adapter of at least 45 W.
2. Install the Acme Home app and sign in.
3. Tap Add Device and scan the QR code on the base of Device Alpha.
4. Choose your Wi-Fi network. Device Alpha supports 2.4 GHz and 5 GHz networks.

## Factory reset
To factory reset Device Alpha, hold the reset button on the back for 10 seconds until the light turns white.
