# Device Beta (model B200)

Device Beta is Acme's portable Bluetooth speaker, model B200.

## Battery and charging
The battery lasts up to 18 hours of playback. Device Beta charges through USB-C at 5 V / 3 A; use a cable rated for 3 A. A full charge takes about 3 hours.

## Water resistance
Device Beta is rated IPX7: it survives immersion in up to 1 metre of fresh water for 30 minutes.

## Warranty
Device Beta comes with a 12-month standard warranty.

## Connectivity
Bluetooth 5.3. Two Beta speakers can be paired as a stereo pair.
