# Troubleshooting

## Device Alpha status light
- Solid white: working normally.
- Blinking amber: no internet connection. Check the router, then restart Device Alpha.
- Solid red: a firmware update failed. Hold the reset button for 10 seconds to restore the previous firmware.

## Device Beta will not charge
Use a USB-C cable rated for 3 A and an adapter of at least 15 W. Do not charge Device Beta below 0 °C.

## Device Gamma screen flickers
Update Device Gamma to firmware 2.4 or later, and check that it is powered by a 65 W adapter.
