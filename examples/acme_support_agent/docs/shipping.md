# Shipping

## Delivery times
Standard shipping takes 3 to 5 business days. Express shipping takes 1 to 2 business days and costs $15, except in Eastport, where express is not offered.

## Tracking
Every order gets a tracking number by email when it ships. Order status can also be checked by order number with Acme support.

## Costs
Shipping costs depend on the region; see the regional policy.
