# Returns

You can return a product within the return window of the region where you bought it (see the regional policy). Products must be returned with all accessories and the original packaging.

## Refunds
Refunds go back to the original payment method within 7 business days of Acme receiving the return.

## Restocking fee
Opened Device Gamma units carry a 10% restocking fee. No other product has a restocking fee.

## Holiday purchases
In Northvale, purchases made between 15 November and 31 December can be returned until 31 January.
